# `pair check` Query Assessment

Status: `MONITOR / DO NOT CREATE A TARGET PAGE`

Assessment date: 2026-08-03

Owner: Founder / Growth

## Signal

Google Search Console has shown Pairvu for the query `pair check`, with impressions and no recorded click at the time of founder review. This is a useful discovery signal, but impressions alone do not establish relevant demand, stable intent, or a page opportunity.

Current public-search evidence is highly ambiguous:

- the broad result set includes dictionary meanings of `pair`, relationship pairing, educational Pairs-Check methods, patent-status terminology, footwear authentication, and technical uses;
- Google autocomplete for `pair check` expands toward `pair check valve`, `pair check method`, `pair check meaning`, `pair check kagan`, and `pairs check strategy`;
- autocomplete for `pair checker` expands toward primer, font, minimal-pair, color-pair, bracket, and key-pair tools;
- `pair check image`, `pair check product`, and `image pair checker` did not produce a stable suggestion cluster in the 2026-08-03 check.

## Interpretation For Pairvu

The impression is most plausibly an exploratory or decomposed query match caused by Pairvu's brand and its repeated explanation that the product compares an image pair. It is not currently evidence that searchers use `pair check` to mean AI product-image fidelity QA.

Pairvu should not create `/pair-check`, rename the homepage category, or dilute `AI product image checker` around this query. A dedicated page would have unclear user intent, weak evidence, and high risk of becoming thin or attracting irrelevant traffic.

## Current Action

1. Keep `pair`, `compare`, and `check` in natural product explanations where they accurately describe the two-image workflow.
2. Do not add `pair check` to title tags, H1s, navigation labels, or repeated anchor text.
3. Keep the homepage focused on `AI product image checker` and the Check family focused on attribute-level intents.
4. Monitor the exact GSC query weekly without treating impressions as success.
5. Record the landing page, country, device, impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position for the query.

## Reassessment Gate

Reconsider the decision only after at least 28 days and one of these conditions:

- the query receives 100 or more impressions with average position 30 or better;
- related relevant variants such as `pair check images`, `product image pair check`, or `check image pair` begin appearing in GSC;
- the query generates a click, checker start, or analysis completion that demonstrates product-fit intent;
- the live result set develops multiple image-comparison or visual-QA pages rather than unrelated meanings.

If the gate is met, first improve the homepage wording or add a concise glossary section explaining Pairvu's reference-candidate image pair. Do not create a standalone route until the query has a distinct user job and enough unique evidence to pass the existing page-quality standards.
