## Why This New Product Family

The Product Components flagship must not repeat the serum bottle, shampoo pump, cleaner sprayer, beverage can, toothpaste tube, food box, or laundry pouch already used elsewhere. A candle set introduces a jar, fitted lid, and long-form accessory whose presence can be judged independently from logo, label text, color, count, and container shape.

The page will not be published until four real Pairvu runs collectively represent PASS, REVIEW, and FAIL. The mixed FAIL deliberately changes both the included accessory and identity-bearing set wording; the page must state that visible text supplies the critical failure signal under the current M0 policy. Component-only additions or omissions remain REVIEW unless a future founder-approved policy explicitly changes that behavior.
