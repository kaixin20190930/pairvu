# Pairvu External Distribution And Backlink Plan

Status: `READY_TO_EXECUTE`

Start date: 2026-08-17

## 1. Objective

Earn qualified discovery, product usage, and legitimate references by sharing
Pairvu's first-party product-image comparisons. A backlink is useful only when
the surrounding audience has a plausible need to check AI-generated product
content.

Do not buy links, submit to mass low-quality directories, exchange sitewide
links, use private blog networks, or repeat exact-match anchor text. Social
links may be nofollow and still be valuable for discovery and product learning.

## 2. Destination Map

| Story or question | Primary destination |
| --- | --- |
| What Pairvu does | `https://pairvu.com/` |
| Why AI product photography needs QA | `https://pairvu.com/ai-product-photography` |
| AI changed a product logo | `https://pairvu.com/checks/product-logo` |
| AI changed product color | `https://pairvu.com/checks/product-color` |
| Product is cropped or not observable | `https://pairvu.com/checks/product-image-observability` |
| Concrete before/after result | The matching `/examples/*` page |
| Pre-publish workflow | `https://pairvu.com/guides/ai-product-photography-checklist` |
| Product action | `https://pairvu.com/#checker` |

Never send every post to the homepage. Link to the page that completely answers
the post's question, then let that page's internal links lead to the checker.

## 3. Channel Priority

### P0: founder-led distribution

1. LinkedIn founder profile: three evidence-led posts per week.
2. X: concise before/after thread or single finding, two to three per week.
3. Existing product audiences around Moxion, Sigoo, AI Best Tool, and Triptrace:
   one contextual mention where the audience overlaps, never a sitewide
   keyword-rich link scheme.

### P1: communities

1. Indie Hackers: build-in-public result, failure pattern, or pricing lesson.
2. Relevant Reddit communities: answer an existing question first; include a
   Pairvu case only when rules permit and it materially helps the answer.
3. Product photography, ecommerce creative, DTC, Amazon seller, Shopify, and
   generative-image communities: share a checklist or controlled case, not a
   generic launch advertisement.

### P1: relationship links

1. AI product photography agencies and freelancers.
2. Product-image generation and editing tools with complementary workflows.
3. Ecommerce operations newsletters, podcasts, and practitioner blogs.
4. Shopify/Amazon creative consultants and brand-content teams.

Send five personalized outreaches per week. Offer a useful controlled case,
guest workflow, or co-tested example. Do not ask for an unexplained link.

### P2: launch and directories

Use Product Hunt and a small number of maintained SaaS/AI directories only when
the listing provides a real product description, canonical website link, and a
relevant audience. Do not pursue directory volume as a ranking strategy.

## 4. Content Formats

Rotate four reusable formats:

1. `One change, one consequence`: approved image, AI candidate, Pairvu verdict,
   and why it matters.
2. `False alarm avoided`: background, lighting, shadow, or repositioning changed
   while the product stayed faithful.
3. `Honest REVIEW`: crop, reflection, tiny text, or viewpoint prevents a safe
   PASS.
4. `Workflow lesson`: the reference-to-candidate checklist before publishing.

Each post needs one actual observation. Avoid unsupported benchmark claims,
generic AI prose, and identical cross-post copy.

## 5. UTM Standard

```text
utm_source=linkedin|x|reddit|indiehackers|producthunt|partner_domain
utm_medium=social|community|referral|launch
utm_campaign=seo_evidence_2026q3
utm_content=<case>_<format>_<version>
```

Examples:

```text
https://pairvu.com/checks/product-color?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=seo_evidence_2026q3&utm_content=color_review_v1

https://pairvu.com/checks/product-image-observability?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=seo_evidence_2026q3&utm_content=crop_review_v1
```

Use lowercase ASCII and stable names. Do not add UTMs to internal links.

## 6. Anchor And Link Rules

Prefer natural anchors in this order:

- Pairvu or `pairvu.com`;
- a sentence-level contextual phrase such as `compare the approved product with
  the AI candidate`;
- the case title;
- a plain URL.

Avoid repeatedly forcing `AI product image checker` as anchor text. The brand
and natural language should dominate earned links.

| 1 | Logo comparison, color change, false-alarm background | 5 agencies/creatives | Referral visits and checker starts |
| 2 | Label value, missing component, observability REVIEW | 5 AI image tools | Which stories earn replies/saves |
| 3 | Batch QA workflow, product-count case, pre-publish checklist | 5 ecommerce practitioners | Completed checks and signups |
| 4 | Best-performing case rewritten with new insight; founder learning post | Follow up with responders | GSC query/page and conversion review |

## 8. Measurement

For each source and landing page, monitor:

- referral sessions;
- `checker_started`;
- both uploads completed;
- analysis completed or failed;
- feedback submitted;
- account signup;
- paid conversion;
- Search Console impressions and queries;
- identifiable AI-assistant referrals;
- earned mentions and links.

Judge a channel by qualified actions and learning, not link count alone.
